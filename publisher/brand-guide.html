Brand direction

Anagnosi by mudon. The open book and its horizontal opening connect the brand to Anagnosi's adjustable reading windows. The feature graphic develops that idea as a clear band across a page instead of repeating a giant app icon.

RoleColour
Evergreen#174D3C
Cool paper#F2F5F3
Soft sage#E3EEE8
Reading ink#172A23

These are the design targets; generated raster pixels include antialiasing and subtle tonal variation. Preserve proportions, clear space and readability at small sizes. The icon is a full-bleed square without a baked outer corner radius or external shadow.

Files and intended use

  • 512 × 512 app icon: Play Store upload; RGBA PNG with opaque artwork.
  • 1024 × 500 feature graphic: Play Store upload; RGB PNG without transparency.
  • Icon generation master: original 1254 × 1254 raster.
  • Feature generation master: original 1794 × 877 raster.

The store exports are dimension/encoding conversions of the generated artwork, with a minimal centre trim for the feature ratio. No marketing claim was added during export. A separate Android adaptive launcher foreground/background configuration is not included.

Creation method and prompts

The artwork was generated using the built-in image-generation tool. The original generation prompts are included below so the direction is reviewable and reusable. No trademark-availability search or exclusivity guarantee is implied.

Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production Google Play app icon for Anagnosi, a focused PDF and EPUB reader by mudon. Create ONE final icon only, actual PNG image square 512 by 512 pixels (if generation resolution is larger, keep composition identical and provide the 512 pixel delivery export). Opaque full-bleed solid evergreen #174D3C background, exact square outer corners, no rounded outer container, no outer shadow. One bold beautifully proportioned off-white #F2F5F3 geometric open-book glyph centered with generous safe margins: two gently rising page wings and a small central spine, a single clean horizontal negative-space reading-window slit through the book symbol, suggesting calm focused reading and a subtle A silhouette. Elegant original minimal graphic identity, flat vector-like clean edges, premium but friendly, instantly legible at 32 pixels, balanced optical weight. Restrict to evergreen and off-white, no gradients, no textures, no 3D, no outlines of phones, no letters or words, no badges, no Google branding, no watermarks, no presentation board or mockup. The entire image is the app icon.

Feature graphic prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: final Google Play feature graphic for Anagnosi, a focused PDF and EPUB reader published by mudon. Produce ONE finished landscape banner only, intended delivery 1024 by 500 pixels, aspect ratio 2.048:1, opaque full bleed. Calm refined editorial graphic in the app's evergreen #174D3C, light sage #E3EEE8 and cool neutral #F2F5F3 palette. On the left, exceptionally clean readable sans-serif exact text 'Anagnosi' with the smaller exact line 'A little room to read.' underneath, and a tiny restrained 'by mudon'. On the right, a striking original stylized open book made of broad crisp off-white page planes on soft sage, with one horizontal reading-window band crossing the middle: subtle dimmed text-like line motifs outside the band and clear evergreen lines within it. This is a symbolic book illustration, never a fake screenshot or phone. Generous negative space and sophisticated balance; all meaningful text and the core illustration inside the central 80 percent width and 76 percent height, keeping at least 80 pixels left/right and 50 pixels top/bottom free of essential content. A slight sense of layered paper may add depth but no grain, no busy decoration, no heavy gradients, no stock photo, no additional icons, no giant duplicate app icon. No third-party logos, no Google Play badge, no device imagery, no watermarks, no promotional pricing, no word 'Free', no ranking claims. Spell the three supplied text strings exactly. Deliver the actual marketing graphic, not a presentation sheet.
