Full description

Make a little room to read.

Anagnosi brings your PDFs and EPUBs into a calm reading space with adjustable reading windows, useful text actions and a reading/rest timer. Start with your own DRM-free books or the included samples. No Anagnosi account is needed.

FOCUS ON THE PAGE
Place clear reading windows over a dimmed page. Move the whole window, resize it with the corner handle and adjust the dimming. Finish editing to hide the handles and return to reading. PDFs support zoom and scrolling in normal reading mode.

KEEP YOUR THOUGHTS CLOSE
Select text for Highlight, colour choices, Copy, Add note, Translate or Read aloud. Use a repeating palette or one colour. Save bookmarks and quoted notes, search the book and navigate its contents. Organise your shelf with tags, favourites and reading status.

LISTEN AND UNDERSTAND
Read selectable text with an installed offline Android voice and adjust the speed. Translate selected passages on your device using Google Translate through ML Kit. Missing language models download on first use, over Wi-Fi by default. Google ML Kit also processes technical SDK and usage information as explained in the Privacy Policy.

READ AT YOUR OWN PACE
Adjust EPUB text size, typeface, spacing and margins. Set reading and rest periods with local timer alerts. Your imported books, notes and reading positions stay in app-private storage; there is no mudon cloud library or account sync.

PLEASE NOTE
Scanned PDFs need a selectable text layer for text tools. DRM-protected EPUBs are not supported. Offline speech needs an installed voice for the chosen language. Translation models require an initial download and device storage. Automated translations can contain errors. Android settings can delay or silence timer alerts. Keep important originals and copies of your notes.
