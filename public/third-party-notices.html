Google Translate and ML Kit

Anagnosi uses Google Translate through the Google ML Kit on-device Translation API. Language models are downloaded when needed. The app's translation help describes model downloads and Google's SDK data practices.

Android text to speech

Speech is provided by a compatible installed Android text-to-speech engine and an available offline voice. Voice packages, pronunciations, availability and their licence terms are controlled by that engine's provider. Anagnosi does not bundle or sell voice recordings.
