Delete your data

Practical ways to remove books, reading records and support correspondence.

Publisher: mudonEffective / updated: 18 October 2026

No account is needed, so there is no Anagnosi account to delete. Most reading information is stored only on your device. Use the options below to remove it directly.

Delete one book and its reading records

  1. Open Library in Anagnosi.
  2. Open the book's options menu.
  3. Choose the delete option.

This removes the app's imported copy and that book's saved position, zoom, highlights, bookmarks and notes. Your original PDF/EPUB outside Anagnosi remains. Other books and shared reading preferences remain.

Delete selected notes, bookmarks or highlights

Open the book and go to Reading tools → Notes or Bookmarks to remove individual items. Use the clear-highlights control in the book's colour settings to remove its saved and temporary colours. Removing an annotation does not alter your original PDF or EPUB.

Text already copied to your clipboard, pasted into another app or exported elsewhere is outside this deletion. Remove those copies in the receiving app or device clipboard controls if needed.

Remove all app-managed local data

In Android, open Settings → Apps → Anagnosi → Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Names vary by device. Alternatively, uninstall Anagnosi.

This removes the app's local library, annotations, preferences, timer state and app-managed downloaded language files, subject to Android's storage handling. Clear cache alone does not remove the whole library. Copies outside the app, including originals and exported notes, are not deleted. The app's configuration disables Android backup, but any copy you made separately must be managed separately.

A fresh start opens an empty library. Import your own PDF or EPUB files to begin reading; no sample books are bundled.

Request deletion of support correspondence

If you emailed mudon, request deletion from muhammadhazim57@gmail.com. Use the same email address where possible and identify the relevant conversation. You do not need to send your books, password or a government ID to make a normal support deletion request.

We will delete information we control or explain any legally justified retention. Resolved support messages are normally removed within 12 months after closure, unless an ongoing dispute, security issue or legal obligation requires longer retention.

mudon cannot remotely erase a library stored only on your device or restore one that has been deleted. The practical device steps above are the way to remove that local information.

Google and other provider records

Google ML Kit can receive technical SDK information and installation-related identifiers. Clearing local app data does not recall information already received by Google. Those records are governed by ML Kit's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy; mudon cannot guarantee deletion of provider records it cannot identify or control.

Your Android speech engine, email provider, website host and any app into which you pasted content may hold information under their own arrangements. For more detail, read the Privacy Policy or contact mudon for help understanding which option applies.
