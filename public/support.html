Reading and importing

Which files can I open?

Anagnosi imports PDFs and DRM-free EPUBs using your device's file picker. EPUB layout is reflowed; some publisher styling is not reproduced. Scanned PDFs remain readable, but word selection needs a text layer.

Can I zoom a PDF while using a reading window?

Yes, in normal reading mode. Finish Move or Resize with Done before pinching, panning or selecting text. The clear window remains, while its numbers and handles disappear.

How does Highlight work?

Select text to open the contextual toolbar. Choose a colour swatch for one colour or Highlight for the repeating palette. Continue colour loop controls whether selections accumulate or replace only the latest temporary selection. Saved colours remain when you turn that setting off.

Read aloud and translation

Why is no voice available?

Install an offline voice for your chosen language in Android's text-to-speech settings. Then select the language in Anagnosi's Reading tools and try again. Voices and settings depend on your device and speech engine. The app does not record your microphone.

Why does translation need a connection?

Missing language models download on first use. Connect to Wi-Fi, or explicitly allow mobile data, and leave enough storage available. Translation text is then processed on device. Google ML Kit also handles technical SDK information; see the translation privacy section.

Can I rely on the output?

Check the original when accuracy matters. Automated speech and translation can make mistakes. They are not a substitute for qualified professional advice or an authoritative translation.

Timer alerts and storage

Why was a timer alert late or silent?

Check notification permission, Do Not Disturb, battery restrictions and whether the app was force-stopped. On Android 12 or newer, allow Anagnosi under Alarms & reminders (or use Enable precise alerts in the Timer tab). Without that access, background alarms are approximate. The open reader also has a direct deadline callback. Deep idle restrictions can still throttle short repeating alarms. Keep a separate timer for important deadlines.

Where are my books and notes?

They stay in Anagnosi's private local storage. There is no account sync or cloud backup operated by mudon. Keep important originals outside the app. See Delete your data before clearing storage or uninstalling.

Why does the app say Update required?

Your installed version is below the supported version and an eligible update has been confirmed. Tap Update now to use Google Play. If it fails, check your connection and storage, then use Check again or Open Google Play. You can close the app and update later, but a confirmed requirement may prevent reading until resolved. Do not uninstall or clear storage to update: that removes app-managed books, notes and progress. If Google Play cannot offer an update, email mudon with your device model, app version and the message you see; no private book is needed.
