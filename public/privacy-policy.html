Privacy Policy

How Anagnosi handles your reading information, provider data and privacy choices.

Publisher: mudonEffective / updated: 18 October 2026

Your reading library stays on your device. Anagnosi does not upload imported books, notes or translation text to a mudon server. Google ML Kit still sends technical and usage information to Google, as explained below.

1. Who is responsible

This Privacy Policy explains how mudon, the publisher of Anagnosi, handles information in the Android app and on these support pages. For privacy or support enquiries, email muhammadhazim57@gmail.com.

Anagnosi is a PDF and EPUB reader with adjustable reading windows, highlighting, bookmarks, notes, a reading timer, offline text to speech and on-device translation. It has no account system, advertising or paid subscription in this version.

2. Information used on your device

The app processes the following information locally to provide the features you choose:

Local reading information
InformationWhy the app uses it
Selected PDFs and EPUBsImport a private copy, read and display pages, extract selectable text, and identify duplicate imports.
Book details and reading activityKeep titles, authors, tags, favourites, reading status, progress, page/paragraph position and PDF zoom.

These records are stored in the app's private storage. mudon does not receive your library or a copy of your reading history. Imported originals outside the app are not changed or deleted by Anagnosi.

Book and library searches are performed on your device. The app does not send search terms to mudon. Clipboard content is handled by Android; another app can receive it if you choose to paste there.

3. Translation and Google services

Translation uses Google Translate through Google ML Kit. Selected text and the translated result are processed on your device. Language models download when needed after you choose to translate, using Wi-Fi by default unless you enable mobile data. A requested download can continue after the translation panel closes.

On-device text processing does not mean that the SDK never communicates over the network. Google's SDK documentation describes collection of technical device and app details, installation-related identifiers, performance and feature-use information, and the configured translation languages. Google uses this information to operate, diagnose and improve its services and address misuse. Network requests also expose routine connection information to the receiving service.

For details of Google's processing and retention, see ML Kit Terms & Privacy, ML Kit Android data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not provide a separate switch that disables all SDK diagnostics. Avoiding translation is not a guarantee that an installed SDK performs no network activity.

4. App updates and version checks

Anagnosi checks for supported app versions when it opens and returns to the foreground. It downloads a small public configuration file over HTTPS from the publisher's update-policy host. The current default is GitHub Pages. Routine connection details, such as your IP address, request time, requested path and network headers, reach that host and may appear in its operational or security logs. Anagnosi adds no book text, notes, reading history, installed-version identifier or user identifier to this configuration request.

The downloaded version policy and a previously verified update requirement are cached in private app preferences. Google Play separately checks update availability and handles installation after your consent. Google's update documentation describes processing of device metadata, the application version, and installed modules or asset packs to determine availability and expected download size. Google Play runs its own processes under its terms. See Google Play updates and data disclosure and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

A confirmed required update can prevent use of an unsupported version, including after cancellation or while offline. First offline use without a previously verified requirement is not blocked. There is no separate switch for version checks. Updating through Google Play normally preserves local reading data; clearing storage or uninstalling removes it.

5. Text to speech and permissions

Read aloud passes the text you select, or the readable passage you choose, to an installed Android speech engine. Anagnosi selects a voice marked as available offline and does not intentionally use a network voice. Speech engines are separate software with their own settings and privacy practices. No microphone recording is needed for reading text aloud.

  • Files: the Android file picker lets you choose the documents to import. This is not permission to upload or browse your entire device.
  • Internet: supports HTTPS version-policy checks, Google Play updates, translation model downloads and SDK network operations.
  • Notifications: if allowed, display reading/rest timer alerts. These are local alerts, not marketing messages.
  • Alarms & reminders: on Android 12 or newer, optional special access lets user-started reading/rest timers use precise alarms. If you decline, background alerts use approximate scheduling. This does not upload reading or timer data.
  • Reboot events: allow the app to restore scheduled timer alerts after the device restarts.

This version does not request camera, microphone, contacts or device-location permission. It does not use a tracking or advertising SDK added by mudon.

6. When you contact us or visit these pages

If you email mudon, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate a problem, manage your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not send a complete private book or sensitive document unless it is necessary and you have the right to share it.

Support email is handled through Google Gmail. The hosting service delivering these pages may process ordinary connection or security logs, such as an IP address, request time and requested page. These pages contain no analytics script, tracking pixel, embedded advertising or contact form, and do not set cookies through their own code. External links and your email provider have their own policies.

7. Who may receive information

Google receives the SDK information described above. Your chosen speech engine handles text for speech. Our email and website hosting providers handle communications or web requests as needed to provide those services. A recipient app receives text only when you choose to paste copied content there.

mudon does not sell your personal information or use your library to target advertising. We may disclose information we actually hold where required by law or necessary to respond to a lawful request or protect rights and security. We cannot disclose a remote copy of a book or note that we do not hold.

Providers may process their information in countries outside your own under their applicable policies and safeguards. The app does not mirror your local library to an overseas mudon server.

8. Retention and deletion

Local books, annotations, preferences and the cached update policy remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Deleting a book removes its app-managed copy and associated reading records; it does not remove your original file. Downloaded translation models can remain for use with other books. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-managed local data, subject to Android's behaviour. Anagnosi disables Android app backup in its current configuration.

We normally delete resolved support correspondence within 12 months after the request closes, unless a longer period is needed for an ongoing dispute, security issue or legal obligation. You may ask us to delete a support conversation earlier.

Google-managed SDK records follow Google's retention arrangements. Removing local app data does not recall technical information already received by Google. mudon cannot promise deletion of provider records it cannot identify or control. See Delete your data for practical steps and the limits of each option.

9. Your choices, security and rights

You can remove books and notes, change reading settings, disable notification permission, restrict network access through Android, or stop using and uninstall the app. A missing language model will prevent translation while offline. There is no Anagnosi account to close and no cloud library for mudon to recover.

App-private storage uses Android's access controls; the app does not add its own password-protected vault or separate encryption layer to your library. Protect your device and keep copies of important originals. Google states that ML Kit's collected SDK information is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, or raise a concern with a data-protection authority. Contact us about information mudon holds. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request, and will explain any limit that applies. Do not send passwords or government identification unless a lawful, necessary verification process specifically requires it.

10. Children and changes to this policy

Anagnosi is a general-purpose reading tool, not a service designed to collect children's personal information. Where local law requires a parent or guardian's permission, that permission is needed before a child provides personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to mudon so we can address it.

We may update this policy when the app, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Where a change needs additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required before relying on that change.
